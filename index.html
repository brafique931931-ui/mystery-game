How I approach a problem

A simple, structured process.

Good analysis isn't just code. It's a repeatable process that starts with a real question and ends with a useful takeaway.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the goal, audience, dimensions, and questions the data should answer.

STEP 02

Prepare

Check data types, missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies before analysis.

STEP 03

Analyze

Use statistics, queries, and visualizations to identify trends and useful patterns.

STEP 04

Communicate

Present the findings clearly and connect them to decisions or next steps.
